## 1 Corridors

### 1.1 Clear width of escape routes

The clear width of an escape route shall be at least 1200 mm, measured at the narrowest point of the route.

### 1.2 Travel distance to a stairway

The travel distance from any cabin door to the nearest stairway shall not exceed 30 m.

## 2 Low-location lighting

### 2.1 Illuminance along escape routes

Emergency lighting shall provide an illuminance of at least 1 lux along the centre line of each escape route.
